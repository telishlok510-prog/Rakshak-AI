## 🚨 THE PROBLEM

### Opening Hook
**"What if one fake SMS could empty an entire family's savings?"**

For millions of first-time digital banking users in rural India, this isn't hypothetical—it's daily reality.


### The Crisis: "Digital Banking Growing Faster Than Digital Awareness"

**The Scam Vulnerability Zone:**
- UPI & Digital Payment adoption curve: Growing exponentially ↗️
- Financial Literacy & Awareness curve: Growing slowly →
- The GAP between these curves = **"The Scam Vulnerability Zone"**

**Key Statistics:**
- ₹1,750+ Crores lost to digital fraud annually in India
- 50,000+ complaints registered monthly
- 65% victims from rural/semi-urban areas
- Only 23% rural citizens can identify scams
- 78% rural users prefer regional languages
- 48-72 hours average scam detection delay

**Real Scam Types:**
1. UPI Payment Scams (fake requests, reverse payments)
2. Phishing SMS (fake bank alerts, KYC threats)
3. Voice Call Fraud (digital arrest, account blocking)
4. Screenshot Scams (fake payment confirmations)
5. QR Code Fraud (malicious payment QRs)
6. Social Engineering (official impersonation)
